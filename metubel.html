Spar in Hyon · Since October 2022

My first responsibilities

I’ve worked at Spar in Hyon since October 2022. When I took a student job at the deli counter, I didn’t imagine I’d still have so much to say about it a few years later.

Customers see me serving them at the counter. By the time someone comes to collect an order, a lot of the work is already done. We’ve checked the stock, worked out the quantities and prepared what they asked for. Doing that week after week taught me why a simple detail, like knowing how much is left, matters as much to the colleague taking over as it does to the customer waiting at the counter.

Above all, those years taught me what it means to be responsible for something. When I tell someone their order will be ready, they plan their day around it. Even a promise made in a conversation that lasts a few seconds matters. That’s a habit I want to take into any team I work with.

My third-year internship

The Metubel jumper

The first thing I remember about my third-year internship is a jumper. On my last day at Metubel, my supervisor took me aside, gave me a company jumper and thanked me. Then he said I was welcome to come back and work during the holidays.

During the internship, people had already told me they appreciated my initiative and how quickly I worked. I’d built a way for the manager to track late departures from the site, and I was glad I could give him a tool he needed. The invitation to come back meant something more personal. The people who had seen me work every day wanted me on the team again.

I went back during the holidays and worked with the co-manager. It went well, and he offered me more work during the academic year. I still work with them alongside my master’s. On the day I received that jumper, I had no idea where the invitation would lead. That’s why I remember it so well.
